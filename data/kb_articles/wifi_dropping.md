# Wi-Fi Dropping or Slow on Corporate Network

Category: Network_VPN

Symptoms: Intermittent Wi-Fi disconnects, slow speeds on corporate SSID, works fine on guest network.

Resolution steps:
1. Forget and rejoin the corporate SSID, confirming the correct WPA2-Enterprise credentials.
2. Update the laptop's wireless adapter driver.
3. Check if the employee is near a known dead zone (see facilities floor map).
4. Toggle airplane mode on/off to reset the radio.
5. If issue is building-wide, escalate to Facilities/Network Engineering for access point health check.
