# Email Not Sending or Receiving

Category: Email

Symptoms: Outgoing mail stuck in outbox, incoming mail delayed, "mailbox full" errors, messages bouncing.

Resolution steps:
1. Check mailbox storage quota and prompt the employee to archive old mail if near the limit.
2. Verify the mail client is online and not in cached/offline mode.
3. Check mail server/transport queue for the domain the employee is emailing.
4. For bounced external mail, check if the recipient domain is on a spam block list.
5. Escalate to messaging team if the transport queue is backed up organization-wide.
