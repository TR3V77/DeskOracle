# Laptop Hardware Failure

Category: Hardware

Symptoms: Laptop won't power on, blue screen crashes, battery not charging, cracked screen, overheating/loud fan.

Resolution steps:
1. Attempt a hard reset (hold power button 10+ seconds) and try a known-good power adapter.
2. Run built-in hardware diagnostics (boot into diagnostics mode).
3. Check warranty/asset tag status to determine if on-site repair or advance replacement applies.
4. For battery issues, check battery health report before ordering a replacement.
5. If diagnostics indicate motherboard or screen failure, open a depot repair or replacement ticket and arrange a loaner device.
