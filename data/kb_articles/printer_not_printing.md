# Printer Not Printing / Offline

Category: Printer

Symptoms: Print jobs stuck in queue, printer shows offline, "spooler error" messages.

Resolution steps:
1. Restart the Print Spooler service on the employee's machine.
2. Clear stuck jobs from the print queue.
3. Confirm the printer is powered on and has network connectivity (ping the printer's IP).
4. Reinstall or update the printer driver if the issue persists after a spooler restart.
5. If the printer itself is offline for the whole floor, escalate to Facilities/Print Services.
